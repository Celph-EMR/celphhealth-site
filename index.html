For clinicians

CELPH

CELPH is an intuitive, FHIR-native electronic medical record for ambulatory care — pediatrics and adults. Workflows are written in clinician-friendly language: we say “Visit,” never “Encounter,” so the chart stays close to how care actually happens.

Today, CELPH supports day-to-day ambulatory practice: a clear schedule, visit documentation, and a behavioral health suite for referrals and follow-through.

An AI medical advisor and an ambient scribe that can draft the visit note as you talk with the patient are in development. Expansion from ambulatory care into specialty and hospital settings is planned.

For patients & families

My CELPH

My CELPH is the patient and family portal — a place to keep a health story together, understand it, and share it when you choose.

You can already follow a timeline of your health journey; upload labs, imaging, and genetic reports; give family members or caregivers access to records they help look after; and share with a provider on CELPH or with any other clinician, for as long as you decide.

Bringing records together automatically from other health systems is coming. AI-assisted, plain-language explanations of what’s on file, and suggested questions to take to your doctor, are in development. Those tools are informational — not a diagnosis, and not a substitute for professional medical advice.
